`country-boundaries.json` is derived from geoBoundaries gbOpen ADM0 simplified boundaries for VNM, LAO,
KHM and CHN (Runfola, D. et al. 2020, "geoBoundaries: A global database of political administrative
boundaries", PLoS ONE 15(4): e0231866), licensed CC BY 4.0. Coordinates were rounded to 4 decimals and
polygon parts far from Vietnam were dropped. The data is coarse: it is used only to reject planned routes
that run deep inside a neighbouring country, never to draw or cut a route.
